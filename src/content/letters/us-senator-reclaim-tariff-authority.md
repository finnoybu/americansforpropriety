**Dear Senator {{rep_last_name}},**

I'm writing as a constituent in {{my_city}}, {{my_state}} to ask you to support legislation restoring Congress's authority over tariffs.

On February 20, 2026, in *Learning Resources v. Trump*, the Supreme Court held 6 to 3 that the International Emergency Economic Powers Act does not let the President impose tariffs. The power to tax imports belongs to Congress under Article I. That should have settled the question. Instead, the administration announced a new 10% global tariff under a different statute the same day, and it has continued to impose sweeping tariffs under national-security authority in Section 232, including 100% tariffs on imported patented medicines for companies that don't strike pricing deals with the White House.

These are taxes on American households and businesses. They were not passed by Congress, and they can change overnight by proclamation. That is not how a tax is supposed to work in a constitutional system.

I'm asking you to:

1. **Support a bill requiring congressional approval** for new tariffs within a short window, with tariffs ending automatically if Congress does not approve them. The bipartisan Trade Review Act, introduced in 2025, is a good model.
2. **Narrow Section 232** so "national security" means national security, with a clear standard and a role for Congress.
3. **Support prompt refunds** to the importers and small businesses that paid the tariffs the Supreme Court struck down.

This is not a partisan question. It is about which branch of government gets to set taxes. I'd appreciate knowing your position.

Thank you for representing our state.
