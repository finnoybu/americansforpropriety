The PRO Act would meaningfully restore the right to organize a union — by ending captive-audience meetings, restoring first-contract arbitration, and overriding state right-to-work laws.

Senate offices count constituent calls. The threshold for an issue to "register" with a senator's staff is shockingly low — often a few dozen calls in a week. Three minutes of your time genuinely moves the needle.

**Suggested script:**

> Hi, my name is [name] and I live in [city, ZIP]. I'm calling to ask Senator [Last Name] to support the PRO Act and to vote against any procedural motion that would block it. I know they've heard from business interests opposing the bill — I want them to hear from a working constituent who supports it. Thank you.
